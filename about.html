Hi, I'm Wenhan

I'm a designer, developer, and lifelong learner based in Vancouver, BC.

I have a bachelor's degree in applied science from SFU’s School of Interactive Arts + Technology, where I explored the space where design meets technology.

Wellness, creativity, and connection are big parts of who I am. I find inspiration in everything from drawing and sculpting to movement, yoga, and even coding — because to me, all of these are just different ways of creating and expressing. They help me stay grounded while exploring new ideas.

At the heart of my work is a simple goal: to create thoughtful, intentional experiences that not only look good, but make sense to my audience.
